# Changelog

All notable changes to Advancements are documented here.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).
Advancements is versioned semantically (`MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`).
It runs on Minecraft 26.3, 26.2 and 26.1 (Fabric + Forge + NeoForge, Java 25) and requires
[Mycel](https://github.com/MyCelium-GG/MyCel-Lib) (`my.celium.org`).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-05

### Added
- Click any advancement in the advancements screen for a small window: how to get it, your progress and every part that is still missing (each biome, food, mob, cat, wolf and frog variant, armor trim). Hover a part you have to see when you got it.
- How to get it: a written explanation for every one of Minecraft's 126 advancements.
- An overlay above the hotbar for one advancement per world or server, with an animation and a chime for every new part and a celebration when it is done. It can also sit in a top corner.
- Works on any server, vanilla ones included: only your game needs the mod.
- Anonymous stats (how often windows are opened, which advancements get the overlay) so we know what to improve. They follow your answer on Mycel's stats screen; nothing else is asked. What is sent: https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/privacy#stats-advancements
